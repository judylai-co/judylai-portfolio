Pendula

Pendula is a Sydney-based customer engagement platform, giving marketing and CX teams the tools to run personalised, multi-channel conversations at scale. I led design end-to-end here as an in-house designer — from the website refresh and design system, to brand collateral and campaign animation.

The problem

As Pendula's focus shifted from mid-market to enterprise, the website no longer reflected the product or spoke to the audience we needed to convert — it had become a genuine drag on lead generation.

The solution

A ground-up refresh of the website and design system — sharper messaging, a bolder visual identity, and a brand guideline the whole team (and future freelancers) could build from confidently.
