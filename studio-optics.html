Studio Optics

Studio Optics is an optometry practice across two Sydney clinics. This is a homepage services redesign concept — reworking how the clinic explains what it offers and why regular eye care matters, ahead of its public launch.

The problem

The services page buried the practice's real strength — in-house diagnostic technology — under generic copy, making it read like any other optometrist rather than a clinic worth choosing.

The solution

A calm, clinical layout that leads with the numbered services, backs them up with real stats on why eye care matters, and answers the practical questions patients actually have.
