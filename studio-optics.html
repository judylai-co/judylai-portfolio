Studio Optics

Studio Optics is a two-clinic optometry practice in Sydney. This relaunch set out to strengthen SEO performance and rework how the clinic explains its services, and why regular eye care matters.

The problem

Studio Optics operated two locations under two different identities, one following Medispecs' minimal black-and-white branding, the other a rushed stopgap built to redirect customers while a proper rebrand waited. Neither site had the in-depth services pages the business needed.

The solution

I led the client through strategic discovery, competitor research, ideal customer, and long-term vision, to build a clear 'why' before any visuals came into play. I then worked closely with them through their own AI-generated concepts, synthesising what truly resonated into one cohesive brand that still felt authentically theirs, before delivering guidelines as their new source of truth. My developer then mapped SEO keywords into a single consolidated website for both locations.
