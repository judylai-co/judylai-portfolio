Jessie's Notes

Indigo Awards 2026

Jessie's Notes is an edu-tech platform designed to help Australian high school students navigate their HSC Maths exams with confidence. I led the brand roll-out and built the full site on Webflow — logo, colour system, UI and launch animation — with no existing guidelines to start from.

🏆 Winner — Indigo Awards 2026, recognised for web design and brand execution.

The problem

With no visual direction and thousands of dollars deep with disappointing results from freelancers, Jessie's Notes needed a holistic identity and web presence that matched the quality of the teaching itself.

The solution

I scrapped the previous design work and, through business-alignment sessions, walked Jessie through a strategic website framework that uncovered the heart of Jessie’s Notes and clarified the vision and mission. From there, I built a friendly, confident brand system that captured Jessie’s Notes, then partnered with a developer to launch a modern, fun Webflow site—a whimsical space for likeminded students preparing for their HSC.
